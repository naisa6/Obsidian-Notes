# Creativity and Problem-Solving

1. **Understand:** Dig past surface-level symptoms to find the root cause. Ask _"Why is this a problem?"_ multiple times.
2. **Ideate:** Generate a wide variety of ideas without filtering or judging them.
3. **Evaluate:** Analyze the ideas, looking for patterns or viable paths forward.
4. **Implement:** Turn the most promising concept into an actionable reality.
